Rules

Game Setup

  • The deck is dealt out evenly to all players, face down — this is each player's draw pile. Nobody looks at their own cards.
  • The first player in the room goes first.

Objective

  • Be the last player left holding cards. Run out of cards on your turn and you're eliminated.

Playing

  • On your turn, click Play to flip the top card of your draw pile face-up onto the center pile.
  • Jack, Queen, King, Ace are power cards. Playing one forces the next player to "beat the challenge" by playing that many plain cards (J=1, Q=2, K=3, A=4) before another power card shows up.
  • If the challenged player plays a power card themselves, the challenge resets to them with a new count — and passes on down the line.
  • If the challenged player runs out their count without playing a power card, the player who played the last power card wins the whole center pile.

Slapping

  • Anyone, anytime — not just on your turn — can slap the pile by pressing the Slap! button or the spacebar.
  • Doubles: the top two cards on the pile are the same rank.
  • Sandwiches: the top card and the card two below it are the same rank (with a different card in between).
  • Slap correctly and you scoop the whole pile onto the bottom of your draw pile.
  • Slap when it's not slappable and you burn the top card of your own pile onto the center pile as a penalty.

Winning

  • The last player with cards left in their pile wins the room.

Strategy

  • Watch the center pile constantly — slapping is the fastest way to build your stack.
  • Don't get slap-happy: a bad slap costs you a card.
  • When you're losing a challenge, root for power cards from other players to bail you out.
  • Sandwiches sneak past distracted opponents — watch three cards deep, not just two.
  • Stacking cards quietly is fine — surviving matters more than hoarding fast.
