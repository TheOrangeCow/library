Rules

Setup

  • The full deck is equally split face-down into each player's pile.
  • No face-up or face-down table cards — just your pile.

Objective

  • Be the first to empty your pile.

On Your Turn

  • You flip the top card of your pile and play it if legal.
  • If it's illegal, it's added to the centre pile and you pick up the whole pile.
  • You may shuffle your pile once per turn before or after flipping.

Special Cards

  • 2 — Reset: Anything can be played next.
  • 3 — Invisible: Acts as the previous non-3 card.
  • 7 — Low: Next player must play 7 or lower.
  • 8 — Skip: Skips the next player. Multiple 8s stack.
  • 10 — Burn: Clears the pile; you go again.
  • A — High or Low: Ace is always played as high (14). If a 7 is active, it plays as low (1).
  • Joker — Rotate: Everyone's pile shifts one place around the table.
  • 4-of-a-Kind Burn: Four of the same rank on top burns the pile; you go again.

Strategy

  • Shuffle before flipping if you suspect a weak card is on top.
  • Save your shuffle — you only get one per turn.
  • When a 7 is active, hope for a low card; shuffle if you think you have one buried.
  • Jokers can save or doom you — they rotate everyone's pile, not just yours.
  • Picking up the pile is brutal — avoid it at all costs late game.
