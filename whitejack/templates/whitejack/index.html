Rules

Objective

  • Get your hand as close to 0 as possible — without ever going below it.
  • You are only playing against the dealer, not other players.

Card Values

  • 2–10: Face value.
  • J, Q, K: Worth 10.
  • Ace: Always worth 1 (never 11).

The Deal

  • Place your bet before cards are dealt.
  • Everyone gets two starting cards. The dealer gets one face up, one face down.

The Swap

  • Before locking in, you get one swap: trade either of your two starting cards for a fresh one from the deck.
  • When you're happy with your two cards, lock in. Their total becomes your starting number.
  • If your locked-in total is exactly 0, that's a White Jack — an instant win paying 3:2.

Your Turn

  • Every card you draw after locking in is subtracted from your total.
  • Hit: Draw a card and subtract its value from your total.
  • Stand: Keep your current total and end your turn.
  • Double Down: Double your bet, take exactly one more card, then stand. Only available right after locking in.
  • If a draw takes your total below 0, you bust and lose immediately.
  • If a draw brings your total to exactly 0, you stop instantly with a perfect hand.

The Dealer

  • After all players have acted, the dealer reveals their hidden card.
  • The dealer must hit while their total is above 4.
  • The dealer stands once their total is 4 or below.

Outcomes

  • Win: End closer to 0 than the dealer — your bet pays 1:1.
  • White Jack: Lock in at exactly 0 on your starting two cards — pays 3:2 (2.5× your bet).
  • Push: Tie with the dealer — your bet is returned.
  • Bust: Drop below 0 — you lose your bet regardless of the dealer.
  • Dealer Bust: Dealer drops below 0 — all non-bust players win.

Chips

  • You start with 1000 chips on registration.
  • Chips carry over between all games and sessions.
  • If you run out, you are removed from the room at the end of the round.

Strategy

  • Use your swap on any card worth 8 or more — high cards are hard to work with when your goal is 0.
  • A locked total of 2–4 is strong: small, safe hits can finish it off.
  • A locked total of 10+ is risky — you'll need several hits, and every one risks overshooting below 0.
  • Stand early once you're within 1–2 of zero unless you know small cards remain in the deck.
  • The dealer keeps hitting above 4, so they're more likely to bust than to land low — let them take the risk if your total is already low.
  • Doubling down is best right after locking in on a small total (2–6), where one more card has a real shot at hitting 0 exactly.
