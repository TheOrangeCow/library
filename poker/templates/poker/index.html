Rules

Objective

  • Make the best 5-card hand using any combination of your 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards.
  • Win the pot by having the best hand at showdown, or by making everyone else fold.

Hand Rankings (Best to Worst)

  • Royal Flush: A K Q J 10 of the same suit — unbeatable.
  • Straight Flush: Five consecutive cards of the same suit.
  • Four of a Kind: Four cards of the same rank.
  • Full House: Three of a kind plus a pair.
  • Flush: Any five cards of the same suit.
  • Straight: Five consecutive cards of any suit.
  • Three of a Kind: Three cards of the same rank.
  • Two Pair: Two different pairs.
  • One Pair: Two cards of the same rank.
  • High Card: No combination — highest card plays.

The Deal

  • Each player is dealt 2 private hole cards face down — only you can see them.
  • Five community cards are dealt face up in three stages: the Flop, the Turn, and the River.
  • The dealer button rotates each hand — the two players to the left post the small blind and big blind.

Betting Rounds

  • Preflop: After hole cards are dealt. Action starts left of the big blind.
  • Flop: Three community cards are revealed. Action starts left of the dealer.
  • Turn: A fourth community card is revealed. Another betting round.
  • River: The fifth and final community card is revealed. Last betting round.

Your Actions

  • Fold: Give up your hand and forfeit any chips already bet.
  • Check: Pass the action — only available when no bet has been made this round.
  • Call: Match the current bet to stay in the hand.
  • Raise: Increase the bet — all other players must call, re-raise, or fold.
  • All-In: Bet all your remaining chips. You can still win up to the amount you put in from each opponent.

Showdown

  • If two or more players remain after the final betting round, all hands are revealed.
  • The player with the best 5-card hand wins the pot.
  • Ties are split equally between the winning players.
  • If everyone folds, the last remaining player wins the pot without showing their cards.

Blinds & Stakes

  • Low: Blinds 10/20 — buy-in 200 to 1,000 chips.
  • Mid: Blinds 50/100 — buy-in 1,000 to 5,000 chips.
  • High: Blinds 250/500 — buy-in 5,000 to 25,000 chips.
  • Your buy-in is taken from your account when you join. Your remaining stack is returned when you leave.


Strategy

  • Play tight preflop — fold weak hands early. Strong starting hands include high pairs (AA, KK, QQ) and big connectors (AK, AQ).
  • Position is power — acting last gives you more information. Play more hands in late position, fewer in early position.
  • Don't always call — if you can't raise, consider whether calling is even worth it. Passive calling leaks chips.
  • Watch the pot odds — only call a bet if the potential win justifies the cost relative to your chance of hitting your hand.
  • Bluff selectively — bluffing works best against one or two opponents, not a full table. A bluff is a story; make sure it makes sense.
  • When the board pairs or brings three of a suit, slow down — someone may have hit a full house or flush.
  • Protect strong hands — bet and raise with good hands to push out players chasing draws cheaply.
  • If you're short-stacked, look for a good spot to go all-in rather than bleed out — pick a strong hand and commit.
  • The big blind gets a discount preflop — you can check if nobody raises, giving you a free look at the flop.
  • Never go all-in out of frustration. Patience is the most underrated poker skill.
