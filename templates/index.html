Games


Pooheads

A fast and chaotic shedding game. Play cards equal or higher than the pile, use special cards to reset, burn, or skip - and whatever you do, don't be the last one left holding cards. Works through your hand, face-up, then face-down cards blindly. Luck meets tactics.

Play Pooheads →

Enraged Pooheads

Pooheads stripped to its violent core. No face-up, no face-down — just a blind pile of cards you flip one at a time. Play it or eat the pile. Shuffle once per turn if you dare. Special cards still wreak havoc, and the Joker rotates everyone's entire pile. Pure chaos, zero safety net.

Play Enraged Pooheads →

Sevens

A calm but cunning sequencing game. Build four suit sequences outward from the 7s - low cards climb toward the Ace, high cards descend toward the King. You can only play what fits, and passing costs you. The first to empty their hand wins.

Play Sevens →

Blackjack

Beat the dealer to 21 without going bust. Place your bet, take a hit or stand - and if you're feeling bold, double down for twice the reward. A bot dealer plays strict basic theory, so the edge is slim. Chips carry over between games, so play smart. You have {{ chips }} chips.

Poker

Texas Hold'em at its finest. Place your blinds, play your hole cards and bluff, call, or raise your way to the pot. Five community cards decide your fate - but the right bet at the right moment can take it all without showing a single card.

Slots

Pull the lever and pray. Match three symbols to win - cherries pay small, diamonds pay big, and a triple 7 or Joker hits the jackpot. Simple, fast, and dangerously addictive.
