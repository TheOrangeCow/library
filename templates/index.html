Pooheads

1-5 Players Strategy Special Cards

A fast and chaotic shedding game. Play cards equal or higher than the pile, use special cards to reset, burn, or skip - and whatever you do, don't be the last one left holding cards. Works through your hand, face-up, then face-down cards blindly. Luck meets tactics.

Sevens

2-6 Players Strategy Card Sequencing

A calm but cunning sequencing game. Build four suit sequences outward from the 7s - low cards climb toward the Ace, high cards descend toward the King. You can only play what fits, and passing costs you. The first to empty their hand wins.

2-9 Players Strategy Betting

Texas Hold'em at its finest. Place your blinds, play your hole cards and bluff, call, or raise your way to the pot. Five community cards decide your fate - but the right bet at the right moment can take it all without showing a single card.
