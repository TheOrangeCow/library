Rules

Objective

  • Beat the dealer by getting a hand value closer to 21 without going over.
  • You are only playing against the dealer, not other players.

Card Values

  • 2–10: Face value.
  • J, Q, K: Worth 10.
  • Ace: Worth 11, or 1 if 11 would bust you.

The Deal

  • Place your bet before cards are dealt.
  • Everyone gets two cards face up. The dealer gets one face up, one face down.

Your Turn

  • Hit: Take another card.
  • Stand: Keep your hand and end your turn.
  • Double Down: Double your bet and take exactly one more card, then stand. Only available on your first two cards.

The Dealer

  • After all players have acted, the dealer reveals their hidden card.
  • The dealer must hit on any total of 16 or below.
  • The dealer must stand on hard 17 or above.
  • The dealer hits on soft 17 (Ace + 6).

Outcomes

  • Win: Beat the dealer — your bet pays 1:1 (double your bet back).
  • Blackjack: Ace + 10-value card on the deal — pays 3:2 (2.5× your bet).
  • Push: Tie with the dealer — your bet is returned.
  • Bust: Go over 21 — you lose your bet regardless of the dealer.
  • Dealer Bust: Dealer goes over 21 — all non-bust players win.

Chips

  • You start with 1000 chips on registration.
  • Chips carry over between all games and sessions.
  • If you run out, you are removed from the room at the end of the round.

Strategy

  • Always stand on 17 or higher — the risk of busting outweighs the reward.
  • Always hit on 8 or below — you cannot bust and need more value.
  • Double down on 11 — strongest doubling hand, especially if the dealer shows a weak card (2–6).
  • Double down on 10 unless the dealer shows a 10 or Ace.
  • Hit on soft hands (hands with an Ace counted as 11) below soft 18 — you can't bust and might improve.
  • When the dealer shows 2–6 (bust cards), play conservatively — stand earlier and let the dealer bust.
  • When the dealer shows 7–A, assume they have a strong hand and hit until at least 17.
  • Never take unnecessary risks when you're already at 18–20 — the dealer needs to beat you.
  • The dealer hits soft 17, which slightly favours the player — don't panic on a soft 17 yourself.
